Skip the local confirmation prompt. Without `--yes`, the CLI shows the requested target on stderr and asks `[y/N]`. Enter `y` or `yes` to proceed. An empty answer, any other answer, or end of input cancels without sending the destructive request.

Both stdin and stderr must be terminals to confirm interactively. In CI, with redirected input, or with redirected stderr, pass `--yes` explicitly. This also applies to `--body` and legacy `unkey api` paths. Authentication, permissions, and delete protection still apply. See [Confirm destructive commands](/platform/cli/output-and-flags#confirm-destructive-commands).
