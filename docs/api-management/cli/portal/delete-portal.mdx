Delete a [portal](/api-management/portal/overview) and sign out all of its users. Most sessions stop working within about 10 seconds, but some can keep working for up to 5 minutes. The keyspace or app behind the portal isn't touched, its keys keep working, and the slug is free to reuse. Calls `POST /v2/portal.deletePortal`.

To stop new sessions without cutting off the users who already have one, disable the portal with [portals update](/api-management/cli/portal/update-portal) instead.

## Usage

```bash
unkey portals delete --portal=<portal> [flags]
```

## Required permissions

`portal.*.delete_portal` or `portal.<portalId>.delete_portal`. Without it you get a 404, as if the portal didn't exist. If your key can read the portal, you get a 403 that names the missing permission instead. See [Root key permissions](/platform/root-keys/permissions).
