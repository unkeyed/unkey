Unkey bills each workspace separately through Stripe. Everything on this page happens under **Settings > Billing** in the dashboard.

## How billing works

A workspace can subscribe to two products. Both charge the same payment method, and each bills on its own invoice.

| Product | What you get | What you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compute plan | Deployments, plus workspace limits such as log retention, team members, and custom domains | A monthly fee that comes back as usage credits, plus any metered usage beyond them |
| API plan (optional) | A monthly quota of key verifications and rate limit checks | A flat monthly fee |

Without a subscription, a workspace is on the Free plan: 150,000 API requests per month and [Free plan limits](/platform/workspaces/quotas).

<Tip>
  Visit [unkey.com/pricing](https://unkey.com/pricing) for current plans and prices.
</Tip>

## Your plan fee is usage credit

The Compute plan fee is a minimum commitment, not a charge on top of usage. Each billing period, the fee grants usage credits of the same amount, and metered usage draws them down before it costs anything extra. Credits reset every period and don't roll over.

On a $25 per month plan:

| Compute usage | Credits applied | Usage charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $10 | $10 | $0 |
| $25 | $25 | $0 |
| $40 | $25 | $15 |

Stay within your credits and the fee is all you pay.

## Reading your invoices

The Compute invoice issued at the start of each period contains:

- The plan fee for the upcoming period, billed in advance
- Metered usage from the period that just ended, minus that period's credits
- Proration lines if you changed plans mid-period

June's usage appears on July's invoice, already reduced by June's credits. The API plan bills its flat fee in advance on a separate invoice.

To download invoices, click **View invoices** on the billing page. It opens the Stripe portal.

## Do API requests cost extra?

No. The API plan fee buys a monthly request quota, and Unkey never charges per request. Going over the quota doesn't block requests either. The billing page and sidebar show your usage as a percentage of the quota, and if you exceed it month after month, [upgrade the API plan](/platform/workspaces/billing/manage#manage-the-api-plan).
