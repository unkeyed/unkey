A connection links your <Tooltip tip="A Compute app: a deployable service inside a project. Not 'your application' in general.">app</Tooltip> to something it uses. You add connections on the app's **Connections** page.

<Note>
  Connections are in beta. Behavior on this page can change.
</Note>

These connection types are available:

- [App](/compute/networking/private-networking#connect-to-another-app): call another app in the same project over private networking

## What is a connection?

A connection grants your app access to one target. It belongs to your app, not to the target, and it works in one direction. A connection from `web` to `payments` lets `web` use `payments`. It doesn't let `payments` use `web`.

Each connection belongs to one <Tooltip tip="A production or preview environment of a Compute app, not the dashboard label on a key.">environment</Tooltip> of your app. The production and preview environments have separate connections. Each connection type describes what your app receives and how to use it.

## Making changes to connections

Unkey saves your app's connections into each <Tooltip tip="One built and running version of an app in one environment.">deployment</Tooltip> when it creates that deployment. The **Connections** page edits the defaults for future deployments, not the deployments that already run.

- To apply an added, changed, or removed connection, redeploy your app in that environment.
- A running deployment keeps its saved connections until it stops or is deleted. Removing a connection doesn't revoke that access.
- Rolling back to a deployment, or waking a stopped one, uses that deployment's saved connections, not the current defaults.
