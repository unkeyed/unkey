Each workspace has its own members, and each member has one role for the whole workspace. There are no per-<Tooltip tip="A group of apps, usually one per codebase or product.">project</Tooltip> or per-keyspace permissions for dashboard users. Manage members under **Settings > Team**.

## Roles

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | Read and change every resource in the workspace, including keyspaces, keys, identities, rate limits, projects, apps, deployments, domains, environment variables, gateway policies, and log drains. |
| Admin | Everything a developer can, plus manage members and invitations, rename the workspace, manage root keys and portal sessions, set the spend budget, and change billing. |

Developers can see most admin-only settings but can't change them. The workspace name and billing controls are disabled with an "Admin access required" message. **Root Keys** is the exception: it doesn't appear in a developer's sidebar.

## Plans that include team members

Compute Pro, Compute Business, and every paid API tier include team members. On Free or Compute Starter, **Settings > Team** shows **Invite your team** with an **Upgrade** button instead of the member list. See [Plans](/platform/billing/plans).

If the workspace loses team access, Unkey deactivates every membership except the oldest one, which belongs to whoever created the workspace. That happens when a paid API tier ends without Compute Pro or Business, or when a workspace on the free API tier moves from Compute Pro or Business to Starter or cancels Compute.

## Manage members

From the member table on **Settings > Team**, admins can invite people by email address, choose Admin or Developer, change a member's role, and remove members. Other roles see **Admin access required** instead of the table.

Invitations expire. If one shows as expired, send a new one.

Removing a member ends their dashboard access. Anything they created stays, and root keys keep working because they belong to the workspace, not to a person. To leave a workspace, ask an admin to remove you.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step to your sign-in. It belongs to your user account, so one enrollment protects every workspace you sign in to.

Open **Account settings** from the user menu in the top navigation. Under **Security**, click **Set up authenticator app** next to **Multi-factor authentication**. You can enroll even if your organization doesn't require MFA. WorkOS, the identity provider behind Unkey sign-in, runs the enrollment steps.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dashboard/platform--workspace-two-factor-auth--account-settings.png" alt="Account settings page with the Profile section and, under Security, the Multi-factor authentication row with a Set up authenticator app button" />

To switch devices, remove the old factor and enroll the new one on **Account settings** while you can still sign in. If you've lost the device and can't sign in, email [support@unkey.com](mailto:support@unkey.com) from the address on your account.
