Count the requests that the Unkey API served for your workspace per time bucket, split by status class.

The filters have the same shape as in `logs.listApiRequests`, without `requestId`. In one field, values under `is`, `contains`, `startsWith`, and `endsWith` are combined with OR. Values under `isNot` and `notContains`, and the range operators `gt`, `gte`, `lt`, and `lte`, are combined with AND. Different fields are combined with AND. Every text match is exact and case-sensitive.

`filters.time` sets the window. A missing lower bound is the start of your log retention, and a missing upper bound is now. Unkey picks the bucket width from the window. It is the distance between the `time` of two points. The first bucket starts at or before the window start and counts its whole width. Windows reach back at most 100 days, and requests outside the log retention of your workspace are not counted.

## Usage

```bash
unkey logs api-requests metrics [flags]
```

## Flags

<ParamField body="--filters" type="string">
  A JSON object. Filters for the metrics. They have the same shape as the filters of `logs.listApiRequests`, without `requestId`. A missing field does not filter. Different fields are combined with AND.

  <Expandable title="Properties">
    <ResponseField name="time" type="object">
      Filters by the time of the event, in Unix milliseconds. Every bound is combined with AND. Rows older than the workspace's retention are never returned or counted, whatever the bounds.

      <Expandable title="Operators">
        <ResponseField name="gt" type="integer">
          Keep events after this time.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="gte" type="integer">
          Keep events at or after this time.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="lt" type="integer">
          Keep events before this time.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="lte" type="integer">
          Keep events at or before this time.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="path" type="object">
      Filters a text field. Every operator is exact and case-sensitive. `%` and `_` match only themselves. The positive operators (`is`, `contains`, `startsWith`, `endsWith`) are combined with OR. The negated operators (`isNot`, `notContains`) are combined with AND.

      <Expandable title="Operators">
        <ResponseField name="is" type="string[]">
          Keep rows where the field equals one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="isNot" type="string[]">
          Remove rows where the field equals one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="contains" type="string[]">
          Keep rows where the field contains one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="notContains" type="string[]">
          Remove rows where the field contains one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="startsWith" type="string[]">
          Keep rows where the field starts with one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="endsWith" type="string[]">
          Keep rows where the field ends with one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="method" type="object">
      Filters by HTTP method.

      <Expandable title="Operators">
        <ResponseField name="is" type="string[]">
          Keep rows with one of these methods. Values: `GET`, `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`, `HEAD`, `OPTIONS`.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="isNot" type="string[]">
          Remove rows with one of these methods. Values: `GET`, `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`, `HEAD`, `OPTIONS`.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="status" type="object">
      Filters an integer field. `is` keeps rows that equal one of its values. `isNot` and every bound (`gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`) are combined with AND, so `{ "is": [200], "gte": 400 }` matches no rows.

      <Expandable title="Operators">
        <ResponseField name="is" type="integer[]">
          Keep rows where the field equals one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="isNot" type="integer[]">
          Remove rows where the field equals one of the values. At most 10 values.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="gt" type="integer">
          Keep rows where the field is greater than this value.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="gte" type="integer">
          Keep rows where the field is greater than or equal to this value.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="lt" type="integer">
          Keep rows where the field is less than this value.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="lte" type="integer">
          Keep rows where the field is less than or equal to this value.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

## Required permissions

Your root key must have the following permission:

- `unkey:v1:<workspace_id>:logs/apiRequests#read`

See [Root key permissions](/platform/root-keys/permissions).

## Examples

```bash
unkey logs api-requests metrics --filters='{"status":{"gte":400,"lt":600}}'
```

Send the request body as JSON:

```bash
unkey logs api-requests metrics --body='{"filters":{"status":{"gte":400,"lt":600}}}'
```

## API endpoint

The command calls `POST /v2/logs.getApiRequestMetrics` and prints its response. See the <a href="https://github.com/unkeyed/unkey/blob/main/svc/api/openapi/spec/paths/v2/logs/getApiRequestMetrics/index.yaml" target="_blank">endpoint definition</a> for the HTTP contract. Request fields use camelCase in `--body`.
